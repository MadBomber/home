## Statement of Faith

Trusting in Jesus Christ as our Savior, we believe in:

1. The Bible as the inspired Word of God
2. The Deity of our Lord Jesus Christ, God's Son
3. The vicarious death of Jesus Christ for our sins, His bodily resurrection, and His personal return
4. The presence and power of the Holy Spirit in the work of regeneration.

## About LampLight.Guide

> Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.
>
> Psalm 119:105 (ESV)

LampLight.Guide is a free, Scripture-centered platform for publishing and engaging with weekly Bible studies and with essays on the Bible and the Christian life. It is built on the conviction that the Bible is the living word of God — breathed out by Him, and profitable for every dimension of the Christian life:

> All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work.
>
> 2 Timothy 3:16–17 (ESV)

This site exists to help believers do exactly what Paul urged Timothy to do, come to the text with care, humility, and diligence:

> Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a worker who has no need to be ashamed, rightly handling the word of truth.
>
> 2 Timothy 2:15 (ESV)

All Scripture on this site is quoted from the **English Standard Version (ESV)** unless otherwise noted.

## How It Works

LampLight.Guide publishes two different kinds of thing, and they work differently.

**Bible studies** are structured reading plans, organized into sections, weeks, and daily readings. You work through a study a day at a time, and the site remembers where you are and what you have written.

**Essays** are individual articles on Scripture, theology, and the Christian life. Each one can be read on its own. Many also belong to a collection, a set of related essays meant to be read in order. Essays live on the **Essays** page.

Everything you do on this site — notes, journal entries, progress, settings — is stored **only on your device** using your browser's local storage. Nothing is saved by the LampLight.Guide server.

### Bible Study Features

- **Daily Readings** — structured day-by-day study pages with the week's scripture
- **Journal** — a private place to write down what you make of the day's reading
- **Reading Plan** — a full overview of every week and day across the study
- **Memory Verses** — weekly verses to hide in your heart
- **Group Discussion Guides** — questions designed for small group use
- **Study Settings** — control your group email list, export or import your data, and reset progress

### Essay Features

- **Collections** — related essays gathered into a series and ordered so you can read them start to finish
- **Search** — full-text search of every essay. Your search terms stay on your device. The search runs in your browser, not on the server.

### Across the Site

- **Global Settings** — choose your preferred theme, font size, and feature toggles

## Privacy

This site collects no personal data. Your progress, journal entries, and preferences live in your browser's local storage and are never transmitted to the LampLight.Guide server. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy_policy/) has the details, including the outside services that some features depend on and what those companies can see.

## The Template

LampLight.Guide is built on the **Bible Study Template**, an open-source static site template built with [Bridgetown](https://www.bridgetownrb.com/). The template is freely available for churches, small groups, and individuals who want to publish their own Scripture-based studies.
