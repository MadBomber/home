## Reading: Matthew 5:43-48

<blockquote class="passage">
<p><sup data-nospeak>43</sup>“You have heard that it was said, ‘You shall love your neighbor and hate your enemy.’ <sup data-nospeak>44</sup>But I say to you, Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you, <sup data-nospeak>45</sup>so that you may be sons of your Father who is in heaven. For he makes his sun rise on the evil and on the good, and sends rain on the just and on the unjust. <sup data-nospeak>46</sup>For if you love those who love you, what reward do you have? Do not even the tax collectors do the same? <sup data-nospeak>47</sup>And if you greet only your brothers, what more are you doing than others? Do not even the Gentiles do the same? <sup data-nospeak>48</sup>You therefore must be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect.</p>
<cite data-speak-pause>Matthew 5:43–48 (ESV)</cite>
</blockquote>
