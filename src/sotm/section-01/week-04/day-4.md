## Reading: Matthew 6:25-32

<blockquote class="passage">
<p><sup data-nospeak>25</sup>“Therefore I tell you, do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink, nor about your body, what you will put on. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing? <sup data-nospeak>26</sup>Look at the birds of the air: they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not of more value than they? <sup data-nospeak>27</sup>And which of you by being anxious can add a single hour to his span of life? <sup data-nospeak>28</sup>And why are you anxious about clothing? Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin, <sup data-nospeak>29</sup>yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these. <sup data-nospeak>30</sup>But if God so clothes the grass of the field, which today is alive and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, will he not much more clothe you, O you of little faith? <sup data-nospeak>31</sup>Therefore do not be anxious, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we wear?’ <sup data-nospeak>32</sup>For the Gentiles seek after all these things, and your heavenly Father knows that you need them all.</p>
<cite data-speak-pause>Matthew 6:25–32 (ESV)</cite>
</blockquote>
