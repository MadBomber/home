## Overview

A 52-week chronological journey through the Old Testament, organized around the eight covenants that structure God's redemptive plan from creation through consummation.

| Section | Weeks | Theme |

| [Adamic Covenant](<%= relative_url '/ot1y/section-02/' %>) | 3–4 | The fall, the promise, and the line of Seth |
| [Noahic Covenant](<%= relative_url '/ot1y/section-03/' %>) | 5–8 | Judgment, salvation, and the rainbow promise |
| [Abrahamic Covenant](<%= relative_url '/ot1y/section-04/' %>) | 9–16 | Blessing to all nations through Abraham's seed |

| [New Covenant](<%= relative_url '/ot1y/section-07/' %>) | 45–49 | The better mediator foretold by the prophets |
| [Consummation](<%= relative_url '/ot1y/section-08/' %>) | 50–52 | The returning king and the world made whole |
