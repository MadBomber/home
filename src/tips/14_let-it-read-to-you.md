title: "Let It Read to You"
category: site
description: Your browser can read any page, or just the memory verse, out loud.
---

Every page has a Speak button in the toolbar. Click it and your browser reads the page aloud, using its own built-in voice, not a downloaded audio file. Click it again while it is reading and it stops immediately, wherever it happens to be.

The memory verse page has a smaller version of the same idea: a speaker icon next to the reference that reads just the verse, out loud, reference and all.

![The Week 1 memory verse card, with the verse text and a small speaker icon beside the John 1:14 reference.](/images/tips/memory-verse-listen.png)

Good for the two minutes before you have it memorized, when you want to hear it more than you want to read it again.

Both depend on your browser having a voice installed, which almost every phone, tablet, and computer already does. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is downloaded; the words never leave your device. If you commute, fold laundry, or just want to keep your eyes off a screen for a minute, this is what the button is for.
