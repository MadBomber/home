Every study has more paper behind it than the pages you read online. Open Downloads from the toolbar and you will find, for each study, the whole thing as one printable volume and a short reading calendar built just to sit next to your Bible.

![The Downloads page's Study Books group, showing two files: the full study volume and a five-page Reading Calendar handout, each with its file size.](/images/tips/downloads-ntc1y.png)

The reading calendar is the one worth grabbing first. Five pages, one block per week, and inside each block a line for Day 1 through Day 5 naming exactly which chapters or verses that day covers, for the whole year, laid out as a quick reference instead of buried one week at a time inside the site.

Print it and keep it in your Bible, or just save the PDF to your phone. Either way, you can see the whole year's readings before you start, or check tomorrow's passage without opening a laptop. The full volume is there too, every week's readings, context, and questions in one file, for whenever you want the entire study offline instead of the page-by-page version online.
