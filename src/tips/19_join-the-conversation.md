---

Turn on Community Discussions in Settings and a comment section appears at the bottom of every study page: one page, one thread, tied to that specific day or week so the conversation stays attached to what it is actually about.

![The Discussion section at the bottom of a day page, showing a reaction row, a comment count, and a Sign in with GitHub button.](/images/tips/discussions-section.png)

The comments themselves live on GitHub, not on this site's own server, and posting requires a free GitHub account. That is a real bar to clear, and it is there on purpose: it keeps the conversation attached to real accounts instead of anonymous drive-by comments. Everything posted is public, visible to anyone who opens that page, so write the way you would in a room with people you have not met yet.

If you would rather read a passage in silence, leave the toggle off. The study works exactly the same either way; the comments are an option, not a requirement.
