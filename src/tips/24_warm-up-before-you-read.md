description: An opt-in quiz asks a few quick questions about the weeks you have already read, right before you start a new one.
---

The hardest part of a year-long reading plan is not the reading. It is coming back. Between the end of one week and the start of the next, the thread goes slack, and a new week opens with names and places you half remember from three weeks ago.

The New Testament study has a warm-up quiz for exactly that moment. Turn it on and every weekly overview from week 2 onward begins with a small line that asks: Remember? Follow it and you get eight quick questions drawn from the weeks you have already read. Multiple choice, one question at a time, a minute or two start to finish.

Nothing is graded and nothing is reported. The only thing the site keeps is your own personal best, stored in your browser like everything else here. Miss a question and the quiz tells you the answer in words, names the passage it came from, and links you straight back to that day's reading. A wrong answer that sends you back to the text is the quiz doing its job.

![A quiz question, 1 of 8: John's Gospel opens by calling Jesus "the blank". Four choices are listed, and the choice Word is outlined in green. Below the choices, in green, the verdict Correct! Under that, a line reads: That's from John 1:1. Read it again: Week 1, Day 1.](/images/tips/quiz-reveal.png)

The quiz is off by default. Open Settings from the menu, find Features, and switch on Warm-Up Quiz.

Take it before you read, not after. Reaching back for last week's story just before you pick up this week's is what makes the year hold together as one book instead of fifty-two pieces.
