title: "Pray After You Read"
category: study
description: Understanding is not the finish line. Ask what to do about what you just read.
---

Understanding a passage is not the finish line. It is the starting line for the harder question: now what?

Before you close your Bible, ask again, this time for something different. Not open my eyes to see what is here. Show me what to do about it. A passage you understand but never act on has done you no good at all.

James says the same thing in plainer terms.

> But be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves.
>
> James 1:22 (ESV)

Hearing without doing feels like progress. It is not. It is a way of letting the Word pass by without cost, and it deceives you into thinking you have grown because you have understood.

So ask for one thing you can actually do today, this week, with the people already in your life. Not a vague resolve to be more faithful. One specific obedience, small enough to actually finish. That is what turns a study into a life.
